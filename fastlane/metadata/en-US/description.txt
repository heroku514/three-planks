Three Planks keeps one pretend gap on the phone. It starts open. Put the left plank down and the right plank down. The middle plank goes across only when both sides are down. A side stays down while the middle is across. Lift the middle, and a side can come up again. The huge line says the gap is open, one side is down, it is ready for the middle, or the bridge is ready.

The gap stays on this phone. Start a new bridge when you want every plank up again.

Three Planks does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. It does not cross a real gap. The planks in the app are original.
